# Political “insider traders” — people, vehicles, last 48 hours

Compiled 2026-08-20. This is a research file, not a charge sheet.

**Legal frame.** The STOCK Act (2012) already makes it illegal for members of Congress to trade on material non-public information from their official duties, and it requires disclosure of trades over $1,000 within 45 days. Almost nobody on this list has been convicted of insider trading. They are *famous for* large, well-timed, or high-volume STOCK Act disclosures, spouse/trust routing, or (in a few cases) a federal investigation that later closed. Only a handful of former members were actually convicted. Names below are included because they are the ones the press, trackers, and “copy Pelosi” apps treat as the political-trading set.

Sources used: New York Times / 2iQ analysis (reprinted 2026-08-14), STOCK Act trackers (InsiderFinance, Capitol Trades, AltIndex, GovGreed, Kapitol), House clerk PTR PDFs, SEC Form 4 / EDGAR, Reuters, ProPublica, Vox, ABC News, CNN, Axios, NOTUS, AL.com.
