# Index-entry watchlist — S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100, next 180 days

As of 20 Aug 2026. Horizon: through ~16 Feb 2027 (covers the **September 2026** and **December 2026** reconstitutions; the March 2027 S&P window is just outside 180 days).

This is a **flow / eligibility** note, not a buy list. Index membership does not change free cash flow. It changes who is forced to own the stock. A value investor treats inclusion as a possible mechanical bid and still requires a margin of safety on the business.

## How the gates actually work

### S&P 500 (committee, not a robot)

S&P Dow Jones Indices, *S&P U.S. Indices Methodology*; market-cap bands last updated in the 1 Jul 2025 press release:

- Unadjusted company market cap **≥ $22.7B** for *additions* (reviewed each calendar quarter; 85th percentile of the S&P Total Market Index).
- Security-level float-adjusted cap ≥ 50% of that threshold (~$11.35B).
- U.S. company, U.S. primary listing (NYSE / Nasdaq / Cboe).
- Positive GAAP net income in the latest quarter **and** over the trailing four quarters combined.
- Liquidity: annual dollar volume / float ≥ 0.75; ≥ 250k shares/month for six months.
- Seasoning: typically **12 months** post-IPO (fast-track IPOs exist for very large deals; not the base case here).
- **Committee discretion** on sector balance, dual-class, and whether a name “belongs.” Meeting the screens is necessary, not sufficient.

Scheduled membership changes usually effective the **third Friday** of March / June / September / December. Off-cycle adds fill M&A holes (Ferguson replaced EA on 5 Aug 2026; Reddit replaced AvalonBay on 18 Aug 2026).

**KBW (via CNBC, 13 Jul 2026):** Q3 discretionary changes **announced 4 Sep 2026**, **effective 18 Sep 2026**. KBW expected merger-related vacancies in the S&P 1500, including at least one S&P 500 seat.

### Nasdaq-100 (rank-based, non-financials only)

Nasdaq *NASDAQ-100 Index Methodology* (post 1 May 2026 updates):

- 100 largest **non-financial** Nasdaq-listed companies by **full market cap**.
- Banks / brokers / insurers / most fintechs classified as financials **cannot** enter (so HOOD, SOFI, RKT, AFRM, COIN are S&P stories, not NDX stories).
- Quarterly rank review (Mar / Jun / Sep / Dec): drop names outside the **top 125**; fill from the largest eligible non-members. Buffer 101–125 keeps incumbents.
- **Fast Entry:** a new Nasdaq listing that would rank in the **top 40** of current NDX members can be added after ~15 trading days (7-day ranking reference).
- Annual reconstitution still in **December**.

June 2026 already used the new quarterly membership review: added ALAB, CRWV, NBIS, RKLB, TER; dropped CHTR, CTSH, INSM, VRSK, ZS (Nasdaq IR, 11 Jun 2026, effective 22 Jun).
