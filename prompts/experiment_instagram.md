Identify the top 5 stocks where institutional buying has accelerated in the last 90 days but retail attention is still low.

I want stocks where smart money is loading up before the crowd notices.”

2. EARNINGS WHIPLASH MAP

Prompt:
“For the next 14 days of S&P 500 earnings reports, identify the 10 stocks with the highest historical post-earnings volatility (8%+ moves up or down).

Cross-reference each with current implied volatility from the options market.

Flag the 3 names where implied volatility is meaningfully lower than historical realized volatility.

Output the move expected vs. the move historically delivered.

These are the asymmetric setups.”

3. SECTOR ROTATION DETECTOR

Prompt:
“Compare the past 30 days of S&P 500 sector performance against the same period one year ago. Identify which sectors are showing leadership rotation (relative strength flipping from negative to positive).

Then pull the 5 highest-volume ETFs in each rotating sector and rank them by money flow over the last 10 trading days.

I want to see where institutional capital is quietly moving before sector rotation becomes obvious in headlines.”

4. INSIDER + OPTIONS FLOW CONFLUENCE

Prompt:
“Pull SEC Form 4 insider purchases over $500,000 from the last 30 days where the executive is buying their own company’s stock with personal funds (not stock grants).

Cross-reference each name with unusual options activity from the past week using Quiver Quantitative or Cheddar Flow data.

Output the 5 stocks where insiders AND options whales are positioning bullishly at the same time.

This combination is rare and historically precedes major moves.”
